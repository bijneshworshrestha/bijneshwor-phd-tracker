## Why this sweep happened

The CBS Strategy & Innovation call (found 27 Sept) showed that advertised, salaried
positions whose topic only *partly* matches the proposal can still be worth applying to,
when there is no GMAT/GRE and the pay is full. This was a one-off sweep of open calls
under that looser topic filter.

**Scope agreed with Bij:** advertised vacancies only, any country, as long as the
position is funded.

**Hard filters applied:** fully funded / salaried; no GMAT or GRE; MBA not explicitly
excluded; no local-language requirement; deadline not yet passed.

**Coverage:** AcademicPositions (management PhDs + country lists for NO, SE, BE, DE, FI,
NL, CH, DK, AT), AcademicTransfer, the CBS and KU (Copenhagen) job boards, and Varbi via
search. Only lightly covered: Germany, Switzerland, Austria (most posts need German), UK
studentships, and a few department boards that could not be fetched (Hanken, ERIM, SDU,
AAU, Aarhus BSS listing was empty).

**Caveats:** Salaries are as stated in each posting; Numbeo cost-of-living floors have
not yet been calculated for Amsterdam, Delft, Tampere, Jönköping, Kuopio or Leuven, so
"funding" above is only a first screen. No self-calculated ECTS figures anywhere
(standing rule) — where credits matter, ask the contact person directly.
